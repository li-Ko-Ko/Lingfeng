将整个 piano-small 文件夹复制到 Lingfeng.exe 同级 synths/，在设置→音源与音色重新扫描。
Copy the complete piano-small folder into synths beside Lingfeng.exe, then rescan in Settings.
Six presets: natural and bright Kawai acoustic piano, Yamaha CP80, Rhodes, Wurlitzer EP200 and Pianet T.
The acoustic bank retains its two velocity layers. Rhodes is a compact single-velocity CC0 GM version. Large concert-grand samples are an optional separate pack.
Reproduce: python scripts/build_piano_packs.py --download --pack small
Source pins, modifications and output hashes are in source.json and files.sha256.json.
