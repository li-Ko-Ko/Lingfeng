PFSynth · 物理建模钢琴 / Physically modeled piano

音源来自 John O’Laughlin 的 PFSynth，以琴弦泛音、击弦瞬态、制音器和弦共振模拟钢琴。
可使用力度、延音踏板（含半踏）、选择延音踏板和弱音踏板。
支持伶风的实时播放、试听和离线音频导出。原始钢琴模型不响应 MIDI 弯音。

PFSynth by John O’Laughlin models string partials, hammer onset, dampers and
sympathetic resonance. It responds to velocity and to sustain (including half
pedal), sostenuto and soft pedals. The original piano model has no pitch bend.
Use it in Lingfeng for playback, audition and offline audio export.
